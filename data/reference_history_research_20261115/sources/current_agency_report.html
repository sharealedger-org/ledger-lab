Report Help

This report lists all Virginia state entities that have been assigned an official agency code. There are certain commissions and boards that don't have their own code and won't be listed. Some of these entities are actually housed and budgeted for under other official agencies.

Hints and Tips

  • By default, only "active" agencies in the current budget are listed. Select "All" under the active menu to get all codes that have ever been used. Codes are seldom ever reassigned, however, agency names do sometimes change.
  • You can open the "Filter" and display many more columns than are normally listed by default.
  • The filter will also let you select any of the agency groups that are used in some places in the PB system and in reports. There is also an option to see all agencies assigned to a selected DPB budget analyst.
  • Try typing in a short string like "health" in the agency name filter and resubmitting the report. You will get any agency that has that string anywhere in the title.
  • You can resort the table by clicking on any of the column names.
  • The Prgms and Funds link will take you to other reports showing all Programs or Funds that are assigned to the agency.
